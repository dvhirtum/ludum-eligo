# Signing in and out

Ludum Eligo uses Google accounts. There are no passwords.

## Signing in

1. Open the app. Visitors who are not signed in see the landing page.
2. Choose **Sign in with Google** and complete Google's consent screen.
3. You return to your collection.

The first sign-in creates your account, with your Google display name and an empty collection. Later sign-ins use the same account. If your Google name changed, the new name is shown.

If you cancel at Google, or Google reports an error, you return to the landing page with the message "Sign-in did not complete".

## Staying signed in

You stay signed in across page reloads and browser restarts for 14 days after your last visit.

## Signing out

Choose **Sign out** in the toolbar, next to your name. The session ends and you return to the landing page.

## Privacy

Each user only sees and changes their own data.
